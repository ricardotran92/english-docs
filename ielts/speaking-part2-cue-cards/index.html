    CUE CARDS¶

    Dưới đây là BỘ 50 CUE CARD IELTS SPEAKING PART 2, mỗi cue card kèm một bài mẫu 2 phút được “lèo lái” tự nhiên về AI/ML/DL/Cybersecurity/Research — đúng theo chuyên ngành & CV của bạn. Bạn chỉ cần học cấu trúc là nói được mọi chủ đề.

    ⭐ CẤU TRÚC CHUẨN CHO MỌI CÂU TRẢ LỜI PART 2 (Bạn nên thuộc)¶

    (1) Giới thiệu ngắn – trả lời đúng đề 1–2 câu
    (2) Mở rộng chi tiết – bối cảnh, cảm xúc, ví dụ
    (3) Câu PIVOT – “Interestingly, this relates to my field in AI…”
    (4) Kéo về AI/ML/DL/Security – liên hệ rất tự nhiên
    (5) Kết luận – 1 câu tổng kết

    🎯 BỘ 50 CUE CARD & TRẢ LỜI XOAY VỀ AI/ML/DL/SECURITY¶

    1. Describe a hobby you enjoy¶

    I enjoy reading research papers and experimenting with AI projects. It helps me relax and learn something new. Interestingly, this links to my field in machine learning and cybersecurity. I often test new models or read about novel architectures, which later inspire my research. This hobby supports both my academic growth and future career in research.

    2. Describe a place you like to study¶

    I love studying in quiet cafés. The peaceful environment helps me concentrate. Interestingly, many of my AI research ideas were born there—especially when I was working on malicious PDF detection. So cafés are not just relaxing but also creative spaces for my research.

    3. Describe a friend who influenced you¶

    A close friend encouraged me to return to university to pursue IT. Interestingly, this decision shaped my AI research path—now I work with deep learning, security, and image processing. He played a major role in my academic journey.

    4. Describe an important decision you made¶

    Choosing to pursue a second Bachelor's degree in IT was life-changing. Interestingly, it opened the door for me to explore AI, publish Q1 papers, and shift into research. This decision completely reshaped my future.

    5. Describe a useful app or website¶

    I use Google Colab a lot. It’s convenient for running deep learning experiments. As someone working in AI, it helps me test models quickly without worrying about hardware. It’s an essential part of my daily workflow.

    6. Describe a skill you want to learn¶

    I want to learn advanced reinforcement learning. It’s becoming important in cybersecurity for anomaly detection and autonomous defense systems. Mastering it will strengthen my research.

    7. Describe a piece of equipment you often use¶

    My laptop is the device I use most. I train deep learning models, code in Python, and run cybersecurity tools on it. It’s basically the center of my academic life.

    8. Describe a movie you enjoyed¶

    I enjoyed “The Imitation Game.” It’s about Alan Turing, a pioneer of computer science. It reminds me of how foundational ideas in computing paved the way for today’s AI systems. It motivates me in my research work.

    9. Describe a book you read recently¶

    I read a book on deep learning theory. Interestingly, it helped me understand model interpretability, which is important in cybersecurity. It expanded both my knowledge and research perspective.

    10. Describe something you learned recently¶

    Recently, I learned new methods for detecting malicious PDF files using machine learning. This was directly related to my research and helped me publish a Q1 paper. It was one of the most valuable lessons I’ve acquired.

    11. Describe a project you worked on¶

    I worked on a model that detects malicious PDF files. It combined feature engineering and ensemble machine learning. This project strengthened my interest in cybersecurity AI.

    12. Describe your favorite teacher¶

    My AI professor explained complex concepts in simple ways. It inspired me to pursue lecturing as well. Teaching and researching AI is now my long-term plan.

    13. Describe a time you helped someone¶

    I helped my colleagues at HEINEKEN with SAP system training. Interestingly, teaching made me realize I enjoyed explaining technical concepts—similar to teaching AI today. It shaped my soft skills a lot.

    14. Describe a successful person you admire¶

    I admire Andrew Ng. His work in democratizing AI education inspires my dream of becoming a lecturer. He shaped how I view learning and teaching.

    15. Describe a time you solved a problem¶

    I once fixed a data inconsistency issue using Python automation. This experience led me to think more about data processing, which is essential in AI pipelines. It sparked my interest in machine learning.

    16. Describe your dream job¶

    My dream job is to be a researcher and lecturer. I want to contribute to AI security research and teach the next generation of students. It’s meaningful and aligns with my passion.

    17. Describe a memorable trip¶

    I visited a tech exhibition in Ho Chi Minh City. There were many AI and robotics demos, which reminded me how fast the field is evolving. It was inspiring.

    18. Describe a piece of technology you want to try¶

    I want to try quantum computers. They may revolutionize AI and cybersecurity in the future—especially encryption. It’s exciting and full of potential.

    19. Describe a useful lesson you learned at work¶

    I learned the importance of data accuracy while working with ERPs. Later, I realized data quality is crucial for training machine learning models. This connection influenced my research path.

    20. Describe a future plan you have¶

    I plan to pursue graduate studies in AI security and publish more research. It aligns with my goal of becoming a lecturer. I’m preparing for it through current research projects.

    21. Describe a time you were proud of yourself¶

    Publishing my first Q1 paper made me extremely proud. It was about malicious PDF detection using ML. This achievement confirmed my passion for research.

    22. Describe a habit you want to develop¶

    I want to read at least one research paper per day. This helps maintain my knowledge in deep learning and cybersecurity. It’s essential for my academic growth.

    23. Describe something you waited a long time for¶

    I waited a long time to return to university for IT. The wait was worth it because now I'm fully immersed in AI research. It changed my life.

    24. Describe a person you enjoy talking with¶

    I enjoy talking to my AI classmates. We often discuss models, data, and cybersecurity issues. These conversations are both fun and educational.

    25. Describe a time you changed your mind¶

    I switched from industry work to academic research. Understanding AI’s potential made me rethink my career. It was a major mindset shift.

    26. Describe something you’re good at¶

    I’m good at analyzing data and building machine learning models. These skills come from both my industry experience and research work. They define my academic strength.

    27. Describe a rule you think is important¶

    I believe in practicing data security rules. Even simple habits prevent cyber risks. It’s essential in digital life.

    28. Describe a difficult challenge you faced¶

    Understanding deep learning math was challenging. But working through it helped me become a better researcher. It made me persistent.

    29. Describe something you often think about¶

    I often think about how AI can improve cybersecurity. Malware is evolving, and AI-based defense will be necessary. It's a direction I want to pursue further.

    30. Describe a subject you find interesting¶

    I find machine learning fascinating. It’s mathematically elegant and practically powerful. It’s the core of my research life.

    31. Describe a childhood dream¶

    I dreamed of working with computers. Now I’m studying AI and cybersecurity—so it somehow came true. It’s satisfying.

    32. Describe a gift you received¶

    I once received a programming book. It sparked my curiosity in coding and later AI. It was meaningful.

    33. Describe a time you were busy¶

    During my research deadlines, I was extremely busy training models and writing papers. These moments taught me discipline and time management—vital for AI research.

    34. Describe a good law in your country¶

    I appreciate laws about cybersecurity and data privacy. They encourage safer online behavior and align with my research interests. It’s crucial in the digital era.

    35. Describe a speech or talk you listened to¶

    I listened to a talk by Geoffrey Hinton about deep learning. It expanded my understanding of neural networks. It was inspiring.

    36. Describe a tradition you like¶

    I enjoy Tết (Lunar New Year). It’s a break where I can reset—and sometimes brainstorm new research ideas. A balanced life helps creativity.

    37. Describe a famous product from your country¶

    Vietnam is known for its strong IT outsourcing. It inspires young people to explore computer science. This ecosystem supports my AI ambitions.

    38. Describe a useful object you own¶

    My external SSD stores datasets, code, and research papers. It helps me organize my AI projects efficiently. I rely on it a lot.

    39. Describe a good service you experienced¶

    A cloud computing service I used offered great GPU support. It accelerated my deep learning experiments. Good infrastructure boosts productivity.

    40. Describe something that surprised you¶

    I was surprised by how powerful transformer models are. They revolutionized many AI fields, including NLP and security. It changed my research direction.

    41. Describe a small business you like¶

    I like small tech start-ups focusing on AI solutions. They are innovative and reflect the future I want to build. I learn from their creativity.

    42. Describe a useful invention¶

    I think GPUs are one of the most impactful inventions. They enabled deep learning breakthroughs. I use them almost every day.

    43. Describe a person you want to learn from¶

    I want to learn from researchers in cybersecurity AI. They combine two fields I love. It would be an ideal mentorship.

    44. Describe something you lost¶

    I once lost a USB containing old code. It taught me the importance of backups—especially in AI projects. A small mistake, big lesson.

    45. Describe a historical event you find interesting¶

    The invention of the computer is fascinating. It laid the foundation for modern AI. Without it, none of my research would exist.

    46. Describe a time you felt excited¶

    I felt excited when my research paper got accepted in a Q1 journal. It was about malicious PDF detection. This achievement encouraged me to pursue more research.

    47. Describe something that motivates you¶

    Seeing AI solve real-world problems motivates me. It shows the potential impact of my research. It keeps me going.

    48. Describe a job you would like to do in the future¶

    I want to be a lecturer and AI researcher. Teaching and exploring new knowledge is meaningful. It’s my long-term vision.

    49. Describe a change you want in your life¶

    I want more time to conduct research. AI evolves quickly, and I want to stay ahead. Better time management will help.

    50. Describe an achievement you’re working toward¶

    I’m working toward publishing more impactful AI security papers. This will help me prepare for graduate studies and a research career. It’s my main focus now.
