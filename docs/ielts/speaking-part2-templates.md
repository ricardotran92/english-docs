## ⭐ **PHẦN 1 — TEMPLATE CHUẨN PART 2 (BAND 6.5–7.0)**

## ⭐ **TEMPLATE (Bạn cần thuộc lòng 80%)**

**1️⃣ Opening (trả lời thẳng đề)**

 “I’d like to talk about `<topic>`, which is something I find quite `<feeling>`.”

**2️⃣ Describe basic details (3–4 câu rất dễ)**

 “It’s a kind of `<object/event/place>`. I first saw/used/experienced it when `<time>`.  
 It looks/sounds/feels `<description>`.  
 People usually use/see/do it in `<context>`.”  

**3️⃣ Transition Sentence (câu bẻ lái dễ nhất hành tinh):**

 “What’s interesting is that it sometimes reminds me of my work/study in AI and machine learning.”

**4️⃣ AI-Link (đoạn kéo sang AI – đơn giản, không học thuật quá)**

 “Because I study AI/ML, I often think about how `<AI concept>` works in real life.  
 For example, `<example from your study or paper>`.  
 So even though it’s just `<original topic>, it somehow connects to the way AI deals with <pattern/noise/data/problem>`.”

**5️⃣ Ending (1 câu kết nhẹ)**

 “That’s why this `<topic>` stays in my mind.”

## ⭐ **PHẦN 2 — 2 BÀI MẪU FULL (Level 6.5–7.0, đơn giản, dễ nói)**

---

### ⭐ **Bài mẫu 1 (dễ) — Chủ đề: “Describe a piece of clothing you dislike”**

I’d like to talk about a type of clothing I really dislike, which is a tight formal shirt.
It’s the kind of shirt people wear for business meetings or formal events. I used it a lot when I was working before, and it always felt uncomfortable. The fabric is stiff, and I feel like I can’t move naturally when I wear it.

What’s interesting is that it reminds me of something in AI. Because I study machine learning, I often look at simple things from a more “technical” point of view. A tight shirt makes me think about **limitations**, just like how a machine learning model performs badly when the data is limited or restricted.

For example, in one of my research projects, I worked on detecting malicious PDF files. If the dataset was too small or too “restricted,” the model couldn’t learn meaningful patterns. A tight shirt is similar — it limits movement the way poor data limits a model’s performance.

So even though it’s just a piece of clothing, it connects to the way I think about AI and data.
That’s why I never enjoy wearing it.

---

### ⭐ **Bài mẫu 2 (dễ) — Chủ đề: “Describe a plant in your country”**

I’d like to talk about the lotus, which is a famous plant in Vietnam.
It grows in lakes and ponds, and people love it because it’s simple but beautiful. I saw it many times when I was young, especially during holidays. The flower has a soft pink color, and it looks very peaceful.

What’s interesting is that it sometimes reminds me of the way AI models work. Because I study machine learning and deep learning, I often compare things in nature to patterns in data. The lotus grows in a messy environment, but the flower still looks clean and stable.
This is similar to a good AI model: even when the data has noise or messy information, the model can still find useful patterns.

For example, when I worked on image-processing research, the data wasn’t always perfect, but deep learning still managed to extract good features.
So the lotus makes me think of **robustness**, which is very important in AI.

That’s why this plant is meaningful to me.

## ⭐ PHẦN 3 — **20 CHỦ ĐỀ SIÊU KHÓ (VERSION DỄ – 6.5–7.0)**

Mỗi chủ đề có bài mẫu rút gọn theo template (chỉ 6–8 câu, đủ 2 phút khi bạn nói chậm tự nhiên).

---

#### 1. **Describe a smell you dislike**

I’d like to talk about the smell of burning plastic, which I really dislike.
It’s sharp and makes me feel stressed.
What’s interesting is that it reminds me of “bad data” in AI.
Because I study machine learning, I often compare smells or sounds to noisy data.
Burning plastic feels like the kind of noise that damages model performance.
That’s why this smell sticks in my mind.

---

#### 2. **Describe a type of weather you don’t like**

I really dislike extremely humid weather.
It makes everything sticky and uncomfortable.
Interestingly, it reminds me of how unstable data affects machine learning models.
In high humidity, nothing feels stable—just like noisy datasets.
So this weather connects to how I think about AI data quality.

---

#### 3. **Describe an old object in your home**

I have an old calculator from my first degree.
It still works but looks outdated.
What’s interesting is that it reminds me how fast technology evolves—especially AI.
From simple calculators to deep learning models, things changed quickly.
This object always reminds me why I love tech.

---

#### 4. **Describe a crowded place you don’t like**

I don’t like crowded bus stations.
They’re noisy and unpredictable.
This chaos reminds me of raw, unfiltered data in cybersecurity.
When everything is mixed together, it’s hard to detect threats—just like spotting danger in a crowd.
So this place always stresses me out.

---

#### 5. **Describe a time you waited a long time**

I once waited months for my research paper review.
The process was slow and stressful.
But it taught me patience, which is important when training ML models.
Some models take days to train, so waiting is normal.
That’s why this experience helped me grow.

---

#### 6. **Describe a picture or painting you dislike**

I saw an abstract painting with random shapes.
I couldn’t understand it at all.
It reminded me of adversarial examples in deep learning—images that confuse AI models.
Sometimes humans see nothing, but AI gets fooled.
So this painting made me think about that concept.

---

#### 7. **Describe a sound you find annoying**

I don’t like the sound of constant tapping.
It breaks my focus.
Interestingly, it reminds me of signal noise in AI.
When training models, noise can destroy accuracy.
So this sound always annoys me.

---

#### 8. **Describe a habit you want to break**

I want to stop working too long without breaks.
When coding or training models, I often forget time.
But taking breaks helps performance—just like early stopping in machine learning.
So I’m trying to change this habit.

---

#### 9. **Describe something you find difficult to use**

I find outdated websites hard to use.
They’re slow and confusing.
They remind me of old machine learning systems that need upgrading.
Good design and usability are important for both AI and websites.
So I always prefer modern interfaces.

---

#### 10. **Describe a festival you don’t enjoy**

I don’t enjoy very loud festivals with fireworks.
The noise is too much for me.
It reminds me of noisy datasets in AI that are hard to clean.
Too much noise makes it hard to focus—both for people and models.

---

#### 11. **Describe an animal you find interesting**

I find ants interesting.
They’re small but very organized.
This reminds me of swarm intelligence in AI, where simple agents work together.
Nature often teaches us how to design better algorithms.

---

#### 12. **Describe something you forgot**

I once forgot an important password.
It caused trouble and delays.
Since then, cybersecurity became more important to me.
I started using better methods like password managers.
It was a simple mistake with a big lesson.

---

#### 13. **Describe a rule you find unreasonable**

At work, there used to be too many approval steps.
It slowed everything down.
It reminded me of slow, inefficient algorithms.
Optimization is important in both work and AI.

---

#### 14. **Describe a time you solved a problem**

I once fixed a Python automation script that kept failing.
It felt great.
It also made me realize how debugging is similar to training ML models—try, fail, improve.
This problem taught me persistence.

---

#### 15. **Describe something you want to learn**

I want to learn reinforcement learning.
It’s useful for robotics and even cybersecurity.
Understanding it would help me go deeper into AI.
It’s challenging but exciting.

---

#### 16. **Describe a piece of technology you want to try**

I want to try a quantum computer.
It may change the future of AI.
I’m curious how it can speed up learning and encryption.
It sounds futuristic.

---

#### 17. **Describe a person who behaves strangely**

I met someone who talked extremely fast and changed topics randomly.
It felt chaotic.
It reminded me of unstructured data in AI that needs cleaning.
That experience showed me the importance of data preprocessing.

---

#### 18. **Describe a small business you like**

I like small tech shops selling electronic parts.
They inspire me to build things.
They remind me of how simple components create complex AI systems.

---

#### 19. **Describe a tradition you don’t understand**

I don’t understand traditions that waste too much money.
It feels unnecessary.
It reminds me of inefficient models that use too many resources.
Efficiency matters everywhere.

---

#### 20. **Describe a mistake you made**

I once deleted an important file by accident.
It was stressful.
It taught me the importance of backups—one of the basics in cybersecurity.
Now I always save copies.
