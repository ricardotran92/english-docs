# OVERVIEW

Dưới đây mình chuẩn bị **một bộ câu trả lời mẫu hoàn chỉnh cho cả 3 Parts của IELTS Speaking**, được **cá nhân hoá theo CV của bạn** , đồng thời tích hợp **kỹ thuật “topic steering”** — chuyển hướng tinh tế mọi câu hỏi (đặc biệt ở Part 2) về những chủ đề bạn mạnh như **Computer Science / AI / ML / DL / Cybersecurity** mà vẫn tự nhiên, không gượng ép.

---

## ⭐ TƯ DUY CHUNG CHO IELTS SPEAKING (Topic Steering)

Bạn có thể **luôn xoay về 3 trục mạnh nhất của mình**:

### **(1) AI / Machine Learning / Deep Learning**

→ Nói về cách công nghệ đang thay đổi lĩnh vực liên quan.

### **(2) Cybersecurity / Data Security**

→ Nói về rủi ro, bảo mật, tầm quan trọng của dữ liệu.

### **(3) Research & Education**

→ Nói về việc bạn làm nghiên cứu, đọc paper, hoặc giảng dạy.

**3 câu thần chú chuyển hướng khéo**:

* *“…which reminds me of my research in AI…”*
* *“…and because I work with cybersecurity, I tend to look at it from a technical angle…”*
* *“…as someone who studies AI and data science, I usually think about…”*

---

## PART 1 — *Introductions & Personal Questions*

(1–2 phút | Mẫu trả lời dựa trên CV và định hướng cá nhân của bạn)

---

### **1. What do you do?**

I’m currently pursuing my second Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, majoring in Computer Science. My academic focus is on AI, particularly machine learning, deep learning, and cybersecurity. Before that, I worked for several years in data and system control roles at companies like Samsung Electronics and HEINEKEN Vietnam, where I handled ERP systems and data analysis. Now, I’m working toward a research and lecturing career.

---

### **2. Why did you choose to study IT?**

I chose IT because I’ve always been fascinated by how intelligent systems can solve real-world problems. During my professional work in data systems, I realized that automation and AI could significantly improve efficiency and security. That motivated me to return to university and pursue a second degree focusing on AI and cybersecurity.

---

### **3. Do you prefer studying alone or with others?**

I usually study alone, especially when working on research projects involving machine learning models or reading technical papers. But I also enjoy team discussions—AI research often requires collaboration, such as debugging models, evaluating datasets, or sharing different approaches.

→ *Tự nhiên kéo về AI rất mượt.*

---

### **4. What kind of technology do you use daily?**

I mostly use my laptop for coding in Python, training deep learning models, or experimenting with cybersecurity tools. I also rely on cloud services and research platforms like Google Colab, GitHub, and academic databases.

---

### **5. Do you enjoy your field of study?**

Absolutely. AI and cybersecurity are fast-growing and meaningful fields. What I enjoy most is that my work can be applied to real-world problems—example, one of my research papers focuses on detecting malicious PDF files using machine learning.

## PART 2 — *Cue Card* (2 phút nói liên tục)

Dưới đây mình tạo **5 cue card mẫu bất kỳ** cùng phiên bản trả lời có **topic-steering AI/cybersecurity**. Bạn có thể dùng cấu trúc này cho **mọi chủ đề khác**.

---

### ⭐ TEMPLATE DÙNG CHO MỌI CHỦ ĐỀ

(Cứ áp vào là nói được)

**1. Short description (trả câu hỏi) →**
**2. Expand (story, feelings, background) →**
**3. Shift to AI/cybersecurity (dùng 1 câu pivot) →**
**4. AI/tech angle (phân tích, liên hệ nghiên cứu) →**
**5. Wrap up**

Câu pivot gợi ý:

* *“Interestingly, this connects to my research in AI…”*
* *“From a cybersecurity perspective…”*
* *“As someone who works with machine learning models, I see this differently…”*

## 🎯 *Cue Card 1:* Describe a hobby you enjoy

### **Sample Answer (AI-steered Version)**

One hobby I really enjoy is reading research papers and experimenting with small AI projects. I started this habit a few years ago when I was working in data systems, and I realized how powerful machine learning could be in solving daily problems.

Interestingly, this hobby connects closely to my study and research orientation. For example, when reading papers on deep learning, I often get new ideas for my own projects, such as my study on detecting malicious PDF files with machine learning. It helps me stay updated and improves my technical skills in cybersecurity.

Overall, this hobby is meaningful because it supports both my personal interests and my long-term goal of becoming a researcher and lecturer.

---

## 🎯 *Cue Card 2:* Describe a place you like to visit

### **Sample Answer**

A place I really enjoy visiting is quiet cafés. I often go there to study, read, or work on machine learning experiments. The calm atmosphere helps me think clearly.

This actually relates to my field because I often use these moments to brainstorm research ideas, whether for deep learning in medical imaging or cybersecurity topics like malware detection. Many of my best ideas came when I was sitting in a café debugging code or analyzing a dataset.

So even though it’s just a simple place, it has supported a lot of my academic progress.

---

## 🎯 *Cue Card 3:* Describe a person who inspires you

### **Sample Answer**

One person who inspires me is Andrew Ng. He has contributed significantly to machine learning education and applied AI. What inspires me most is how he makes difficult concepts understandable for everyone.

This strongly aligns with my own career goal of becoming a lecturer and researcher. I want to not only conduct research—like my recent work on malicious PDF detection or medical imaging—but also explain complex ideas clearly to students.

So he motivates me to continue improving my knowledge and teaching skills.

---

## 🎯 *Cue Card 4:* Describe an important decision you made

### **Sample Answer**

An important decision I made was pursuing my second Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology. At that time, I already had a career in industry, working with ERP systems at Samsung and HEINEKEN. But I realized that AI and cybersecurity were the fields I wanted to specialize in.

This decision changed my life. Since then, I’ve published research in Q1 journals and focused on deep learning, medical imaging, and cybersecurity. It also aligns with my future plan to teach and conduct research.

---

## 🎯 *Cue Card 5:* Describe something you learned recently

### **Sample Answer**

Recently, I learned about advanced techniques for detecting malicious PDF files using ensemble machine learning models. This topic became part of my research, and it was exciting because it combines AI and cybersecurity—two areas I’m passionate about.

Learning this helped me understand how attackers modify PDF structures, and how data-driven models can detect anomalies. It also improved my research methodology and deepened my interest in building AI systems for security applications.

## PART 3 — *Discussion (abstract, deeper questions)*

(Ở phần này examiner sẽ hỏi rộng hơn — bạn chuyển hướng về AI/cybersecurity rất dễ.)

---

### **1. Do you think technology makes life easier?**

Yes, absolutely. Technology—especially AI—has significantly improved efficiency in many fields. For example, machine learning automates repetitive tasks, and cybersecurity systems help detect threats faster than humans. However, the more we depend on technology, the more important data security becomes.

---

### **2. How will jobs change in the future?**

Jobs will become more skill-based and technology-driven. Routine tasks will be automated by AI models, while new jobs related to cybersecurity, machine learning engineering, and data science will increase. This is also why I chose to specialize in AI and security—these fields will shape future industries.

---

### **3. Is it important to protect personal data online?**

Definitely. Cyber-attacks and data breaches are becoming more common. In my own research on detecting malicious PDF files, I’ve seen how easily attackers exploit documents. This shows that cybersecurity education and AI-powered detection systems are essential to protect individuals and organizations.

---

### **4. Do you think AI will replace teachers?**

AI will support teachers, not replace them. Intelligent systems can help with grading or personalized learning, but teaching requires human judgment and empathy. Since I aim to become a lecturer, I see AI as a useful tool to enhance education, not a threat.

---

### **5. How can governments encourage innovation?**

They can invest in research, support universities, and create funding for AI or cybersecurity projects. When students and researchers have access to resources, they can develop meaningful technologies—like machine learning applications in healthcare or security.
